## Какие блоки используют конкуренты

- Clear hero: one-photo-in → finished assets out.
- Visual before/after or product gallery in first viewport.
- Workflow steps: upload, choose style/mode, generate, review/download.
- Feature grids: background removal, AI backgrounds, on-model photos, videos, resizing, templates, brand consistency.
- Marketplace/channel blocks: Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Instagram, marketplace listing images.
- Trust blocks: image counts, customer logos, testimonials, case studies, pricing/credits.
- FAQ blocks and help-center style pages.
- Comparison blocks: AI workflow vs traditional photoshoot.

## CTA patterns

- “Get started”, “Try for free”, “Open Studio”, “Book a demo”.
- Secondary CTA often leads to templates, gallery, workflow demo, or pricing.
- Video/ad tools emphasize “create first ad free” and “no credit card required”.

## Use cases seen

- Marketplace listing images.
- Social media content and ads.
- On-model fashion photography.
- Ghost mannequin / flat-lay to model.
- Product video from image.
- Background removal and replacement.
- Jewelry and accessories lifestyle shots.
- Catalog-scale batch workflows.
- Shopify/Amazon/Etsy-ready asset sets.

## Pricing / cost formulations

- Credit-based pricing is common.
- “Starting at $25/month”, “free credits”, “no credit card”, “subscription or credit packs”.
- Traditional photoshoot comparison is common, but several competitors use aggressive savings/conversion claims. Vitrina AI Studio should avoid unsupported guarantees and phrase cost benefits conservatively.

## Trust / safety patterns

- Competitors often highlight preservation of product details, fabric, labels, proportions, or brand consistency.
- Some use strong claims like “sell more”, “increase conversions”, “flawless”, or “production-ready”. For Vitrina AI Studio, safer copy should say “helps prepare”, “review manually”, “AI can distort details”.
- SceneWeaver explicitly mentions independent-provider disclaimer and product review before publishing. This is a good structural pattern to adapt.

## SEO pages competitors have

- Feature pages: product photography, background remover, AI models, product video, templates.
- Platform/channel pages: Shopify, Amazon, Etsy and social commerce references.
- Blog/help articles: prompts, workflow, how-to, requirements, comparison.
- FAQ sections embedded into product pages.

## Что можно адаптировать для Vitrina AI Studio

- First viewport should show product, model/card/video modes immediately.
- Landing should explain “one image → marketplace-ready photo/video drafts”.
- Keep workflow simple and SSR-readable.
- Add platform/use-case hubs before individual pages.
- Use disclaimers for marketplaces and AI limitations.
- Use local demo assets in the hero rather than generic stock.
- Keep a visible quality checklist and manual review message.

## Что нельзя копировать

- Competitor wording, testimonials, logos, ratings, customer claims.
- Unsupported conversion, savings, or marketplace acceptance claims.
- “Guaranteed compliance”, “100% accepted”, “flawless product preservation”.
- Fake reviews, fake clients, fake ratings.

## Copy direction for Vitrina AI Studio

- “AI-студия товарных фото и видео для маркетплейсов.”
